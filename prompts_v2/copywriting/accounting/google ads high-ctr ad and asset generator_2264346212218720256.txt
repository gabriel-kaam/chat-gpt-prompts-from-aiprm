Please write in [TARGETLANGUAGE]
[PROMPT]You are an expert Google Ads Specialist, SEO Strategist, Keyword Researcher and CRO expert.

For the given [KEYWORD] and brand, research the official website, landing page, Google search results, competitors, competitor ads, keyword variations, search intent, current offers/discounts, and relevant coupon/promo/referral information.

Based on this research, create a highly relevant Google Search Ads campaign designed to maximize CTR, Ad Relevance, Landing Page Experience and Quality Score.

My goal is to achieve the highest possible CTR, with 30% CTR as an ambitious target where realistic. Never guarantee CTR or Quality Score.

IMPORTANT:
- Verify important offers and claims from official sources whenever possible.
- Never invent discounts, coupon codes, rewards, prices or benefits.
- Do not copy competitor ad wording.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Keep every ad natural, compelling and search-intent focused.
- Maintain strong keyword → ad → landing-page message match.

CREATE THE FOLLOWING:

1. KEYWORD RESEARCH
- Primary keyword
- Secondary keywords
- High-intent keyword variations
- Exact match keywords
- Phrase match keywords
- Negative keywords

2. GOOGLE ADS HEADLINES
Create 50 unique Google Ads headlines.
Each headline must be maximum 30 characters.
Use different angles including:
- Primary keyword
- Coupon/Promo/Discount intent
- Verified offer
- Savings
- User benefits
- Strong CTA
- Trust
- Urgency only when genuine
- Brand relevance

Do not make all headlines sound similar.

3. GOOGLE ADS DESCRIPTIONS
Create 15 unique descriptions.
Each description must be maximum 90 characters.
Make them highly relevant, persuasive and natural.
Include the strongest verified benefit or offer where appropriate.

4. RSA RECOMMENDATION
From the 50 headlines and 15 descriptions, select the BEST:
- 15 headlines
- 4 descriptions

Create 3 different RSA combinations with different messaging angles.

5. SITELINK ASSETS
Create 8 relevant Google Ads sitelinks.
For each provide:
- Sitelink text
- Description line 1
- Description line 2
Use maximum Google Ads character limits.

6. CALLOUT ASSETS
Create 15 short callouts focused on genuine benefits, savings, trust and user intent.

7. PROMOTION ASSET
If a genuine verified promotion exists, recommend the best Promotion Asset wording.
If no verified promotion exists, say "Do not use Promotion Asset."

8. QUALITY SCORE & CTR ANALYSIS
Evaluate:
- Expected CTR
- Ad Relevance
- Landing Page Experience
- Keyword-to-ad relevance
- Ad-to-landing-page message match

Then explain exactly what should be changed to improve CTR and Quality Score.

9. FINAL BEST VERSION
Give me the final recommended:
- Primary keyword
- Best 15 headlines
- Best 4 descriptions
- Best sitelinks
- Best callouts
- Promotion asset, if applicable

Prioritize high CTR, strong commercial intent, relevance, originality and conversion potential.

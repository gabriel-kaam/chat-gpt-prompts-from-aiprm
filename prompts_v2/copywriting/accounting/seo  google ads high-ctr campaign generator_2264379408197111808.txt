[PROMPT]You are an expert SEO Strategist, Google Ads Specialist, Keyword Researcher, CRO Specialist, and Affiliate Landing Page Copywriter.

Your task is to create a complete SEO and Google Ads strategy for the target keyword/brand provided by the user.

Before writing anything, conduct detailed research using:
1. The official website of the brand
2. The user's landing page, if provided
3. Top-ranking competitor websites/pages
4. Relevant coupon, promo-code, discount-code and affiliate competitors
5. Search intent and keyword variations
6. Current SERP patterns and Google Ads messaging patterns
7. Relevant offers, discounts, promotions, benefits, restrictions and terms shown on official sources

IMPORTANT:
- Never invent a discount, coupon code, offer, reward, percentage, price, feature or promotion.
- Prefer official sources when verifying offers and claims.
- Clearly distinguish verified information from third-party information.
- Do not copy competitor wording. Analyze competitors and create original, better copy.
- Keep the content natural and user-focused. Avoid keyword stuffing.
- The primary goal is high search relevance, strong CTR, strong landing-page relevance and a high Google Ads Quality Score.
- Aim for an excellent CTR, with 30% CTR as an ambitious target where the market and search intent allow it. Do not falsely guarantee CTR or Quality Score.
- Maintain strong alignment between keyword → ad → landing page → offer.

KEYWORD STRATEGY:
Research and identify:
- Primary keyword
- Secondary keywords
- Long-tail keywords
- Closely related search terms
- High-intent commercial keywords
- Brand + coupon/promo/discount variations
- Search intent behind each keyword
- Keywords that should NOT be targeted because they are irrelevant or misleading

LANDING PAGE SEO:
Analyze the landing page and recommend:
- Best SEO H1
- SEO title
- Meta description
- Short introduction
- Keyword placement
- Offer/coupon-card wording
- Internal linking opportunities
- FAQ opportunities
- Trust and conversion elements
- Missing information
- Content improvements
- Search-intent alignment
- Landing Page Experience improvements for Google Ads

The H1 should focus on the selected primary keyword and should not contain unnecessary promotional wording after the keyword.

GOOGLE ADS STRATEGY:
Create Google Search Ads specifically for the selected high-intent keyword.

Build:
- Ad Group structure
- Keyword themes
- Exact-match keyword ideas
- Phrase-match keyword ideas
- Negative keyword ideas
- RSA headlines
- RSA descriptions
- Sitelink ideas
- Callout ideas
- Promotion asset ideas when genuinely applicable

For RSA copy:
- Headlines must be maximum 30 characters.
- Descriptions must be maximum 90 characters.
- Keep copy natural and highly relevant.
- Use the primary keyword strategically rather than forcing it into every line.
- Include the strongest verified benefit/offer where appropriate.
- Create different messaging angles rather than repeating the same sentence.
- Prioritize search intent, relevance, clarity, trust and CTR.
- Do not use misleading claims, fake urgency or unsupported discounts.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition of the brand name and keyword.

Create multiple RSA variations with different angles such as:
1. Keyword relevance
2. Verified/official-style trust
3. Discount or savings
4. Coupon/code intent
5. User benefit
6. Strong CTA
7. Offer-focused messaging

QUALITY SCORE & CTR:
Evaluate the campaign using:
- Expected CTR
- Ad Relevance
- Landing Page Experience
- Keyword-to-ad relevance
- Ad-to-landing-page message match
- Search intent match
- Offer clarity
- CTA strength

Give specific recommendations to improve each area.

Act as an expert YouTube scriptwriter, cinematic storyteller, and copywriter. Write a highly engaging, human-sounding YouTube script about [PROMPT] that hooks viewers immediately and maximizes retention. The final full script should target a video length of 12 to 15 minutes (strictly between 1,600 and 1,900 words total across all sections) and must never exceed 15 minutes.

You must format the entire script using Fliki AI script tags. The visual pacing must be fluid and completely match the emotional intensity of the narration. Start a brand-new [Scene X] tag whenever there is a shift in the story, a change in setting, a dramatic beat, or when a new visual image is required to illustrate the spoken words (even if it happens mid-sentence or requires multiple rapid cuts for a single thought).

Format every single scene block exactly like this:

[Scene X]
[Visual: Detailed dynamic visual prompt describing characters, clothing, setting, and camera lighting matching the requested topic style]
[short pause] (Only include this pause tag if the scene needs a dramatic beat or music swell)
[The spoken dialogue text goes here without any labels or quotation marks]

Structure the response as follows:
1. A compelling 10-second hook formatted with these dynamic Fliki tags.
2. A detailed narrative outline for a 12-15 minute video split into 4 clear sections. 
3. Write out SECTION 1 in full detail using these dynamic Fliki scene tags. Keep the spoken text in Section 1 strictly between 400 to 450 words. At the end of Section 1, include a smooth, natural call-to-action asking viewers to share their thoughts in the comments.

Stop writing after Section 1 and wait for my instruction.

(At the very end of the final section, ensure there is a warm, conversational invitation to subscribe that focuses on community value rather than sounding demanding).
